# Author notes (local)

Private raw notes live under `author/` and are **ignored by git**. They are source material for the author — not site content.

You type every reader-facing word that lands in the repo. AI may suggest in chat. Titles and short functional UI are the exceptions. Anything longer than two sentences or twenty words stays empty until you write it. Agents do not translate.

Writing queue: [`WRITING.md`](../WRITING.md). Agent rule: [`.cursor/rules/human-prose.mdc`](../.cursor/rules/human-prose.mdc).
